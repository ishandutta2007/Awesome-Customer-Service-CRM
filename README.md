# Awesome-Customer-Service-CRM



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Ticketing, Helpdesk, Live Chat, Omnichannel Support & Knowledge Base*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Customer Service CRM**. These tools help support teams manage tickets, resolve customer issues, and deliver omnichannel service across email, chat, voice, and social channels.



**Examples** include Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Salesforce Service Cloud, Zendesk, Freshdesk, ServiceNow CSM, Intercom, Zoho Desk, Gorgias, Kustomer, and Help Scout (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source helpdesk ecosystem is **mature and production-proven**. **Zammad** is the most feature-complete open-source alternative with omnichannel support, SLA management, and enterprise-grade security . **FreeScout** provides a lightweight, Zendesk-style ticketing system with zero dependencies . **osTicket** remains the most widely deployed open-source ticketing system with over 3 million downloads . **Chatwoot** delivers omnichannel customer engagement with AI agent capabilities . This section documents these production-grade solutions.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Customer service CRM platforms handle sensitive customer conversations and PII; ensure compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable data protection regulations.

- **Open-source reality**: The open-source ecosystem for customer service CRM is **mature and production-proven**. **Zammad** is the most feature-complete open-source helpdesk with omnichannel support and SLA management . **FreeScout** provides a lightweight Zendesk-style ticketing system with zero dependencies . **osTicket** remains the most widely deployed open-source ticketing system with over **3 million downloads** . **Chatwoot** delivers omnichannel customer engagement with AI agent capabilities and **37,000+ stars** . However, **commercial platforms** (Zendesk, Salesforce, Intercom) provide **managed infrastructure, enterprise SLAs, and integrated AI agents** that open-source alternatives require significant operational investment to match. The open-source path is **genuinely viable** for organizations with strong engineering capacity seeking full data sovereignty and cost control.

- **Pricing caveat**: All pricing figures above are **verified against cited search results** but may change without notice. **Zendesk's Suite Enterprise** starts at **$169/agent/month** with AI add-ons often adding **$50/agent** . **Freshdesk's advertised $19** grows to **$55–$119** with omnichannel and AI . **Salesforce Service Cloud** add-on stacks typically cost more than the base edition itself . Always request a formal quote and model add-on costs before committing.
